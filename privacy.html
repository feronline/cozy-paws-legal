Cozy Paws Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Cozy Paws is a desktop idle game for Windows, published on Steam by feronline games ("we", "us"). This page says what the game stores, what leaves your computer, and what you can control. We do not run our own game servers and we do not collect your personal data ourselves.

1. What stays on your computer

  • Your save. Your progress (cats, upgrades, settings, window position and language) is stored in a file on your computer under your Windows user profile (AppData\LocalLow\...\Cozy Paws\save.json). It is not uploaded by the game. Deleting the game's data folder deletes your save.
  • Play statistics (optional). If you turn on "Keep play stats on this PC", the game appends one short line per session to a local file on your computer (play-stats.jsonl): game version, date, session length, how far you got, and a few settings. It contains no name, account, machine or file path information. The game does not send this file anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in Options, and you can delete the file yourself.
  • Start with Windows (optional). If you turn this on, the game adds one entry to your user's Windows startup list. Turning it off removes the entry.

2. What Steam handles

Cozy Paws uses Steam features. When you use them, Steam (Valve Corporation) processes your data under Valve's privacy policy:

  • Purchases and items. Fish packs, cosmetics and cats are bought through Steam's checkout and kept in your Steam inventory. We do not see your payment details. Items can be traded or sold on the Steam Community Market under Steam's rules.
  • Leaderboards. If you take part, your best level, stage and phase are sent to Steam's leaderboards together with your Steam name, and other players can see them.
  • Screenshots. If you take a photo in the game, it can be added to your Steam screenshots.
  • Prices. The game asks Steam for the price of fish packs in your currency.

We receive only aggregated information that Steam shows to every game's developers, such as sales and player counts.

3. Our community server

The game has an optional link to our Discord server. If you join, Discord's own privacy policy applies, and its server rules are in the server. The game does not send anything to Discord.

4. Children

Cozy Paws is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. The game does not have accounts or chat.

5. Your choices and rights

Because the game stores your data only on your computer and with Steam, you can:

  • Turn off play statistics, start-with-Windows and the taskbar icon in Options.
  • Delete your save and stats by deleting the game's data folder.
  • Ask Steam about your Steam data, purchases and leaderboard entries (Steam Support).

If you are in the EU/EEA, the UK or Turkey, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, UK GDPR and KVKK (access, correction, deletion, objection). Since we do not hold personal data about you, we will tell you so if you ask; for data held by Steam or Discord, please contact them.

6. Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and its date, and describe the change in the game's update notes.
